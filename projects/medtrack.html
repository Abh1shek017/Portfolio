About MedTrack

MedTrack is a specialized back-office business management application engineered for independent pharmacy owners. Unlike traditional retail Point-of-Sale (POS) interfaces, MedTrack focuses entirely on administrative workflows behind the counter. It manages procurement relationships, tracking complex inventory down to unique batches, and monitors balances with medical distributors.

The application also provides a lightweight Udhaar (credit) ledger to track informal financing extended to local customers. A dedicated B2B module handles wholesale invoicing, business validation checks (Drug License, GSTIN), and records physical stock reductions. To maintain high availability, the app features an offline-first architecture powered by Flutter/Drift SQLite database syncing with a robust FastAPI cloud engine.

Key Modules & Features

  • 📦
    Batch & Stock Management: Track medicine batches with custom expiry dates, MRP, purchase rates, and quantities.
  • 🤝
    Distributor Relations: Log invoices, track outstanding balances, and record partial/full settlement payments.
  • 🏪
    B2B Wholesale Channel: Sell to clinics and minor shops, validate DL/GSTIN credentials, and auto-decrement stock.
  • 📓
    Udhaar Individual Credit: Log simple credits given to trusted individuals and track repayments without customer billing.
  • 💸
    Expense Tracker: Log utility, rent, maintenance, and salary payouts to monitor daily shop cashflow.
  • ⚡
    Smart Sourcing: Create draft orders with automated scoring to suggest the best supplier based on price history.
  • 🔍
    Global Search FTS5: Instant offline results across products, invoices, and customers, categorized by type.
  • 🛡️
    Security Suite: Biometric login locks, Two-Factor Authentication (2FA via email OTP), and active device session revoking.
  • 📄
    Reporting & PDFs: Generate expiry notifications, low stock alerts, spend analysis, and export statements to PDFs.
